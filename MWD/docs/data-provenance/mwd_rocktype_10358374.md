## Publication boundary

The data contain MWD-derived features and geological labels. They do not contain paired field grouting volume, grouting time, Lugeon values, or pressure-flow-volume curves. Therefore results from this repository must be described as MWD-based geological or rock-mass prediction, not as direct field grout-take prediction.
